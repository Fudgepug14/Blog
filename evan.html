Iceland
Rank Up in a Society. This was a game we played with our overimaginative minds. The concept was that I was a beggar on the streets looking for spare change, and Evan would approach me as different people on the street to either bless me up, rob me, offer trades, etc. Shenanigans ensued. We coined the terms centimum (1/10 of a cent) and centitricum (1/100 of a cent). We made a shop on a piece of paper we just kept adding to, and he sent me it in the mail not long ago. We eventually wanted to make it in Minecraft and these are the images I have of it.
