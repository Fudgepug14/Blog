Evan

My friend Evan is a great man. I have known him for many a year and this is an appreciation page for his 19th birthday. Our history is storied and full of bullshit that only really makes sense to the two of us. It would be impossible to document all of it, however, this is a collection of some of my favorite Evan pictures and memories.

Pick your mood setter:

Iceland. This was one of the craziest/most fun time of my life, and my friend Evan was there the whole way. Many great pictures were taken. Notable memories include but are not limited to the penis museum, badger, Evan's fucked up shoes, almost losing Evan on a cliffside, the ice cave incident, the dutch oven incident, those tuna and cucumber sandwiches.
Rank Up in a Society. This was a game we played with our overimaginative minds. The concept was that I was a beggar on the streets looking for spare change, and Evan would approach me as different people on the street to either bless me up, rob me, offer trades, etc. Shenanigans ensued. We coined the terms centimum (1/10 of a cent) and centitricum (1/100 of a cent). We made a shop on a piece of paper we just kept adding to, and he sent me it in the mail not long ago. We eventually wanted to make it in Minecraft and these are the images I have of it.
Just hanging out at his house. I remember this very fondly as a young lad. We would play some game like Mario Maker or Stardew Valley at his house. His Mom would make some really delectable meal and I'd eat 800 apple sauces out of his fridge. I remember having a lot of fun. That's really about it. Fond memories.
Evan's Halloween costume one year. The pretzel was really just perfect with it. I thought it was a really funny costume at the time because it subverted all my preconceptions on what a costume could be. Just a german guy. It was very funny at the time and it still is pretty humorous.
Send Bugs. This is a crazy throwback. We played this game called Animal Crossing Pocket Camp on our phones back in the day, and there was a system where you could grow a garden and send bugs to friends to fulfill requests. Almost every day, we would text and bug each other to send bugs (intentional pun). I honestly can't believe I found these photos it's kinda crazy.
Filming/writing The Andrewverse. This was such a great time and an amazing creative outlet, and it wouldn't have been nearly as good without my buddy Evan. We bounced so many stupid ideas off each other and it resulted in this magnificent video. I'm not gonna put every behind the scenes clip because there is infinite, but here's a funny one.
The pants shirt and shirt shirt incident. I remember Evan got me this gimmick shirt that said 'pants' on it, but he also got a t-shirt of a t-shirt. It was really a great gag. While I remember us coordinating to wear these two shirts at school one day, I thought it was done through normal text messages. Instead, not long ago I found this fossil of a message in my FarmRPG (an old game we played, actually I still play it daily but he has since moved on) mailbox. Pretty cool.
