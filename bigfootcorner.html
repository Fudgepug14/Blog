bigfoot

All my life I have been fascinated by this creature.

They go by many names: The North American Wood Ape, The Swamp Ape, Sasquatch, Skunk Ape, Chiye Tanka, Jo We Ho, Nantinaq, Albatwitch, Yeti, Yowie, or Bigfoot.

The Bigfoot is, in my opinion, the most plausable cryptid to actually exist in some extent.

Through certain pieces of evidence, I have become an unironic believer in Bigfoot.

I'm not here to change minds, but this page will catalogue some cool pieces of evidence and some of the most convincing things I've encountered. I bet you'll find something interesting here.

Exhibit One: Sounds ----------------------------------------------

This is perhaps the most interesting and compelling aspect.

There exist very ominous sounds recorded in the woods with little to no rational explanation for their existance.

- The Sierra Sounds

It is important to note there are a lot of human responses and to distinguish between them.

Spectrogram studies have been done which disprove these sounds could have been produced by a human or other known animal.

Audio engineers report this cannot be a human So what in the world could make such sounds?

This to me is the craziest shit. Even if you don't believe, these sounds are still chilling. One can imagine hearing this alone in the woods and crap their pants.

Bigfoot researchers call this kind of 'language' Samurai Chatter. I think this is a really funny term. I have also heard compelling theories that Bigfoot in the Sierra Mountain Ranges learned to talk like this as they imitate the chinese immigrants building the railroads. I think that's kinda cute. Researcher R. Scott Nelson has phonetically studied these sounds in a very interesting article

- NAWAC Sounds

The North American Wood Ape Conservatory (NAWAC) has some great recordings on their website. check it out!

Some of my favorite ones are #1, 7, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14, 19, 23, 25

Exhibit Two: Images ----------------------------------------------

There won't be a lot here because I'm generally pretty skeptical of a lot of image evidence. I think there are more hoaxes or paradolia than real evidence. I think that real things do exist, but are often low quality and not really worthy of this section. By the nature of photographing a creature which knows about your presence and is trying to be hidden, genuine evidence could very well be low quality. Bob Gyman talks about this type of photo/video evidence very well. I highly recommend watching this video!

The Florida Skunk Ape - The Myakka Photos

Bob Gymlan has a great video on this. I am about 50% inclined to believe that is an orangutan, but there's no doubt this photo is real and quite scary.
