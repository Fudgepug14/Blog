bigfoot

All my life I have been fascinated by this creature.

They go by many names: The North American Wood Ape, The Swamp Ape, Sasquatch, Skunk Ape, Chiye Tanka, Jo We Ho, Nantinaq, Albatwitch, Yeti, Yowie, or Bigfoot.

The Bigfoot is, in my opinion, the most plausable cryptid to actually exist in some extent.

Through certain pieces of evidence, I have become an unironic believer in Bigfoot.

I'm not here to change minds, but this page will catalogue some cool pieces of evidence and some of the most convincing things I've encountered.

Exhibit One: Sounds

This is perhaps the most interesting and compelling aspect.

There exist very ominous sounds recorded in the woods with little to no rational explanation for their existance.

The Sierra Sounds

It is important to note there is a lot of human responses and to distinguish between them.

Spectrogram studies have been done which disprove these sounds could have been produced by a human or other known animal.

Audio engineers report this cannot be a human So what in the world could make such sounds?

This to me is the craziest shit. Even if you don't believe, these sounds are still chilling. One can imagine hearing this alone in the woods and crap their pants.

Bigfoot researchers call this kind of 'language' Samurai Chatter. I think this is a really funny term. I have also heard compelling theories that Bigfoot in the Sierra Mountain Ranges learned to talk like this as they imitate the chinese immigrants building the railroads. I think that's kinda cute.
